Buying a Luxury Home in Weddington, NC: A Buyer's Guide

Weddington has a reputation across Greater Charlotte for spacious homes, generous lots and a countryside feel that's still a short drive from the city. If you're shopping in the luxury price range, it's one of the first places many buyers look. But a high-end home on an acre or more comes with questions that a typical subdivision purchase doesn't. Here's what I walk my buyers through when we tour luxury homes in Weddington.

What makes Weddington different

The town describes itself with the tagline "Rural Living Redefined," and its layout backs that up. According to the Town of Weddington's About page (retrieved November 2026), the town covers about 17 square miles, or roughly 11,000 acres, in Union County, and it "consists almost entirely of single-family homes on lots of one acre or more." Only 27 of those acres are zoned for commercial use, with most shopping and dining clustered in the Town Center near NC 16 and NC 84.

For a luxury buyer, that pattern shapes everything: more space between homes, mature trees, long driveways and room for features like pools, outdoor kitchens, detached garages or workshops. It also means fewer commercial conveniences right next door, so it's worth mapping your regular errands before you fall in love with a property.

Five things to check before you buy

Luxury homes on larger lots reward careful due diligence. These are the items I put at the top of the list:

  • Lot details and boundaries. With an acre or more, it pays to know exactly where the lines are. Review the plat and consider a survey, especially if you're planning a pool, fence, guest house or addition.
  • Water and sewer service. Some properties on larger lots rely on a private well or septic system rather than public utilities. Ask which the home uses, and if it has a septic system, schedule an inspection and confirm the system is sized for the number of bedrooms.
  • HOA and architectural rules. Many Weddington neighborhoods have homeowners associations with architectural review. Read the covenants before you write an offer so you know what's allowed for outbuildings, fencing, exterior changes and landscaping.
  • Zoning and future plans. The town maintains a Unified Development Ordinance that sets lot and building standards by district. If you have plans for the land, check them against the rules for that district early.
  • Systems and upkeep. Larger custom homes often have multiple HVAC zones, irrigation systems, generators and specialty features. A thorough inspection, plus a look at service records, helps you budget for the real cost of ownership.

Financing a higher-priced home

Borrowing costs matter at every price point, and they matter more as loan amounts grow. Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey reported that the 30-year fixed-rate mortgage averaged 7.11% and the 15-year fixed averaged 6.45% as of November 12, 2026. Those are national weekly averages, and your own rate will depend on your loan type, down payment and credit profile.

Many luxury purchases use a jumbo loan, which can have different down payment, reserve and documentation requirements than a conforming loan. Talk with a lender early, get a full pre-approval in hand, and you'll be in a stronger position when the right home comes along. A strong pre-approval also gives sellers confidence in your offer.

Property taxes and town services

Weddington keeps its town tax rate low. The town's About page lists a 2026 town tax rate of 3.5 cents per $100 of valuation. That is in addition to the Union County property tax, so check both when you estimate your monthly costs. On a higher-value home, those numbers add up, and I'm happy to help you pull the figures for any property you're considering.

Schools

Weddington is served by Union County Public Schools. School assignments are based on the home's address and can change over time, so confirm the assigned schools directly with the district for any home you're considering.

Frequently asked questions

How big are the lots in Weddington?

According to the Town of Weddington, the town consists almost entirely of single-family homes on lots of one acre or more. Exact lot sizes vary by neighborhood and property, so always check the plat or a survey.

Do Weddington homes have public water and sewer?

It depends on the property. Some homes connect to public utilities, while others use a private well or septic system. Confirm the setup for any home you're considering and include it in your inspection plan.

Is now a good time to buy a luxury home in Weddington?

The right time depends on your goals, your financing and the homes available. A quick conversation about your plans and a look at current listings is the best way to decide.
