Matthews has a way of surprising people. It sits just southeast of Charlotte in Mecklenburg County, close enough for an easy drive into the city, yet it still feels like a small town with a real main street, a weekly farmers market, and green space woven through its neighborhoods. If you're weighing a move somewhere in the Greater Charlotte area, here's a local look at what day-to-day life in Matthews offers and what to know about its homes.

A historic downtown you can walk

Downtown Matthews is the heart of the town. Its historic streets are lined with locally owned shops, restaurants, and gathering spots, and the scale is walkable rather than built around big parking lots. Longtime landmarks like Renfrow Hardware on North Trade Street sit alongside newer favorites such as Seaboard Brewing and Brakeman's Coffee & Supply. Matthews Station adds more dining in the same compact area.

For homeowners, a lively downtown matters beyond weekend plans. Properties within a short walk or bike ride of shops and restaurants often draw strong interest from buyers who want that convenience, and it's one of the features that sets Matthews apart from purely suburban areas nearby.

The Matthews Community Farmers Market

One of the town's best-known traditions is the Matthews Community Farmers Market downtown. According to American Farmland Trust's farmers market directory, the market runs year-round on Saturday mornings, with a main season from April through November and shorter winter hours from December through March. It's a reliable spot for local produce, baked goods, and plants, and it gives Saturday mornings in Matthews a familiar rhythm. Hours can change, so check the market's current schedule before you go.

Parks, greenways, and time outdoors

Matthews puts a lot of effort into its outdoor spaces, and residents use them year-round.

  • Four Mile Creek Greenway: The Carolina Thread Trail lists the Matthews segment as a paved trail of about two miles, running between trailheads near Matthews Elementary School and Squirrel Lake Park and East John Street. It's popular for walking, running, and biking.
  • Squirrel Lake Park: This park on Pleasant Plains Road has a lake with a dock, a large playground, wooded "fairy trails," and restrooms, and it connects directly to the greenway.
  • Stumptown Park: Right downtown, Stumptown Park hosts outdoor concerts and movie nights during the warmer months, making it a natural community gathering place.

For buyers who value outdoor time, homes with easy access to the greenway or a short walk to one of these parks are worth a close look.

Community events throughout the year

The biggest event on the calendar is the Matthews Alive festival, held every Labor Day weekend. It brings live music, food, and vendors to downtown and draws visitors from across the region. Smaller seasonal events, concerts, and markets fill in the rest of the year, so there's usually something happening close to home.

Getting around

Matthews sits along Independence Boulevard (US-74) with quick access to I-485, which makes it a practical base for reaching uptown Charlotte, Ballantyne, and the rest of Mecklenburg County, as well as Union County towns like Indian Trail, Weddington, and Waxhaw. Commute times vary a lot by route and time of day, so I always suggest buyers test-drive their actual commute during rush hour before committing to a home.

Homes in Matthews

One of the appealing things about Matthews is the variety of housing. Near downtown you'll find older homes with mature trees and established lots. Moving outward, there are traditional brick and siding subdivisions from several decades, many with larger yards. Newer construction and townhome communities add lower-maintenance options. Across Greater Charlotte there are also select luxury properties with custom finishes and generous lots, and nearby areas like Weddington and Marvin offer more estate-style homes if that's your goal.

Because Matthews mixes so many property types, pricing can vary widely from one street to the next. That's why looking at recent comparable sales for the specific kind of home you want matters more than any town-wide average.

Public schools in Matthews are part of Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools. School assignments are tied to each address and can change, so verify the assigned schools for any specific property directly with the district.

Matthews FAQ

Is Matthews part of Charlotte?

No. Matthews is its own incorporated town with its own government, located in Mecklenburg County just southeast of Charlotte.

Is there a farmers market in Matthews year-round?

Yes. The Matthews Community Farmers Market operates on Saturday mornings throughout the year, with reduced winter hours from December through March.
